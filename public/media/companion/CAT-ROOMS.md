Generation brief: four full-body poses in a square 2×2 layout, natural fur coloring, premium soft fuzzy 3D illustration, consistent identity within each breed, palette applied to accessories, no UI, labels or watermark. Each breed's specific poses are listed above. The ragdoll/siamese artwork provided a rendering-style reference. Generation requested transparent backgrounds. Ragdoll and Siamese have alpha; the other four sheets are RGB and are blended at display time using CSS contrast and multiply. Files are retained unchanged. A background-extraction attempt did not produce alpha and is not used in the site.

Room and per-cat pose choices are stored locally on the device. This visual update does not connect music models or change music generation capabilities.
